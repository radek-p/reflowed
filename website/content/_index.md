\pagetitle[ReflowEd]{A \LaTeX{} editor in your browser}
\bigskip
\noindent ReflowEd is a free \LaTeX{} editor that runs in your browser.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{The problem.} With \LaTeX, you compile the whole document
into pages again and again while you are still writing it. Figures move away
from their text, lines run into the margin, and one cryptic error stops
everything.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{What ReflowEd does.} It keeps writing apart from page layout:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No pages.} The text fits the window, wide or narrow, so there
  is nothing to fix while you write.
\item \textbf{Blocks.} Each block is typeset on its own: only the block you
  edit is typeset again, and an error stays in its block.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{ReflowEd is in an early alpha stage.} Expect bugs, missing
features and changes between releases, and keep a copy of anything important
outside the browser.
