\pagetitle[Features]{Your documents}
\bigskip
\noindent ReflowEd keeps your documents in your browser's storage, on your
device. They are not uploaded anywhere: a document leaves your device only
when you export it.

\section*{Documents}
The Documents view lists every document, with a search over their titles,
authors and text. A new document starts blank or from a template: an
article, lecture notes, a problem sheet or a LIPIcs paper. Each document
opens in a tab of its own, and the bar shows when your changes are saved.

\section*{Import and export}
You can import a \texttt{.tex} file, a \texttt{.zip} of a project, or an
arXiv source archive (\texttt{.tar.gz}), by choosing it or by dropping it on
the page. A document is exported as a \texttt{.tex} file, as a \texttt{.zip}
of the whole project, or as a PDF, which the engine in your
browser compiles.

\section*{Versions}
\textsf{Versions} in the document menu keeps named versions of a document,
and restores any of them. Versions are also saved on their own: when you
import a document, before a restore, and every ten minutes while you edit.

\section*{On the home screen}
On a phone or a tablet, Add to Home Screen puts ReflowEd beside your other
apps. Opened from there, it has the whole screen, without the browser's
address bar.
