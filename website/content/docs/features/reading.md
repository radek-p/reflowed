\pagetitle[Features]{Reading and themes}
\bigskip
\noindent Every block is typeset by \TeX, and its lines are broken again
for the width you read at. The text keeps \TeX's fonts, spacing and
formulas at any width.

\section*{View options}
The View options set how the document looks while you work. The column
width is Narrow, Medium, Wide, Paper (the document's own text width) or
Fit, or any width on a slider. The text size goes up and down in steps.
The colours follow your system, or are Light, Dark, Sepia or Contrast,
and the drawn figures follow them too.

\section*{On a phone}
On a narrow screen the text is broken for the width of the phone, so it
reads without zooming. The side panel opens over the document
instead of beside it, and on a touch screen the controls are large enough
to tap.
