\pagetitle[Features]{Sections and the outline}
\bigskip
\noindent A long document is easier to work on a section at a time.
ReflowEd keeps its structure in view and lets you move whole sections
around.

\section*{The outline}
The side panel's Outline lists the document's headings with their numbers.
Click a heading to go there. Drag one to move its whole section, with its
subsections, to another place in the document.
{{< /latex >}}

\section*{Jumping and focusing}
The section menu at the top of a document lists every section, to jump to
one or to open it in a new tab. Focusing a pane on a section shows only that
section and its subsections; numbers and cross-references are still those of
the whole document.
{{< /latex >}}

{{< latex preamble="site" >}}
\section*{Two views of one document}
A tab can be split to the right or down, and both halves can show the same
document: one at the section you are writing, the other at the definitions
you refer to. Both are views of one document, so an edit in one shows in
the other at once.

Focus mode (Command-Shift-F, or Ctrl+Shift+F) goes further the other way:
it hides the side panel and dims every block but the one you are editing.

\section*{Notes and problems}
A \verb|\todo| note shows as a small mark in the text. The TODOs panel lists
every note of the document, by author, and the Problems panel lists errors,
undefined references, unknown citations and duplicate or unused labels.
